System

The responsive system, written down

Colour, type steps and motion were already tokens. Breakpoints, type roles, spacing, shape, elevation and layers were not, so each section grew its own. The full text is design/system/README.md; the rules that change code are these.

Band Width Checked at Composition
Phone under 640 320, 375, 390, 430 One column. The line hangs down a bamboo pole; one Diary page; the postcard's sides stack.
Tablet 640 – 1023 768 Still touch and the Menu tag, but two columns wherever an object has two sides.
Laptop 1024 – 1279 1024 The desktop header and the horizontal line; objects at their compact size; the Diary opens to a spread.
Desktop 1280 and up 1280, 1440, 1920 The boards. Content stops growing at 1440; sky and ground carry on.
  • Three breakpoints, in rem, everywhere: 40, 64, 80. Today there are about 13 values in three unit styles.
  • An object sizes to its own box. The postcard, the card, the form pair and the Diary page switch on container width, not the window.
  • Nine type roles, each one step of the existing scale. The 23 ad hoc pixel sizes map onto them.
  • 12px floor. Today 9, 10, 10.5, 11 and 11.5px exist.
  • Named shape, elevation and layers: three radii, three paper shadows, four z-layers, from the 14 / 16 / 10 values in use.
  • One capture command, pnpm shots: nine widths, both themes, key states, with a before/after diff.

Board 1

About: a line strung between two things that stand in the ground

Redrawn after your first look. Under 1024 the bamboo started and ended in mid-air and the line dropped straight down the page. Desktop already had the answer: a bamboo on one side, a pine on the other, both rooted. Phone and tablet now follow the same rule.

  1. On a phone the words come first: headline, lede, both actions on one row, then the hint, right above the tags it is about.
  2. Two supports stand at the page's edges with their feet in the paddy: two bamboo poles on a phone, bamboo and pine on a tablet. The line crosses between them, three times on a phone and twice on a tablet. Nothing floats and nothing is cut off.
  3. Tags hang from a sagging line on strings of different lengths and swing from where the string meets the line, so no two sit level. The order still reads left to right, top to bottom: 2003, 2018, 2021, 2025, then me. On a tablet the headline sits under the line beside the field tag, and the whole section fits one iPad screen (about 1070px, from 1700).
  4. The ground is one band at the foot of the section: the paddy, Moon's võng, the phin. The poles end in it.

Board 2

Journey: boughs in pairs, and a board that fits its window

The same oak and the same six tags. Two changes, one per problem.

  1. From 640 to 1279 the title, subtitle and "Watch it grow" sit beside the crown instead of above 230 – 330px of bare sky.
  2. Boughs leave the trunk in pairs, a tag each side at up to 340px (from 236px). The section is about 1400px tall at 1024, from 2100.
  3. Bé Nhi's swing takes the last right-hand bough, level with the last tag.
  4. From 1280 the board is laid out in board units (1/1440 of its own width) instead of fixed pixels, so it fits at 1280 and grows to about 1680 at 1920. Type stays in rem. "Watch it grow" moves under the subtitle at every width.

Board 3

Diary: a page as tall as what is on it, and the spread from 1024

  1. Where one page shows (under 1024), the page is as tall as its own content, with a floor of about one screen. The book changes height after a turn lands.
  2. The sticky note follows the content and the arrows follow the note, so they are never 400 – 700px below what you were reading.
  3. At 320 and 375 the tab counts drop out before "Milestones 9" wraps onto the board.
  4. The two-page spread starts at 1024 (two ~450px pages) instead of 1280.
  5. Page 1 picks its arrangement from the page's own width, so 1280 stops showing the phone arrangement on the left page. Contents rows are 44px tall.

Board 4

Contact: two whole lines for email or phone, and a card that turns

  1. Email and phone are two full-width lines tied by one bracket down the side, at every width. One star, one note, as now.
  2. The business card turns landscape when it has 36rem to itself (a tablet), and stays portrait beside the postcard.
  3. The postcard's sides sit side by side only from 40rem of its own width (today 32rem). On a laptop that makes it picture over address, with a 520px form, beside the card.
  4. The Ask is as tall as its promise until the first question, then opens to its fixed window. On a phone that window is up to 60% of the screen instead of 17rem.

Your call

Seven decisions before any code

Each has my recommendation first. A plain "go with your picks" is a complete answer.

  1. Ask: short until the first question? Round 24 fixed the window at one height. Recommended: yes, it opens on the first question. The alternative keeps the fixed height and only rebalances the two sides.
  2. Diary: may the book change height between pages on a phone? Recommended: yes, with a one-screen floor. The alternative keeps one height and I only trim the tallest page.
  3. Diary: spread from 1024? Recommended: yes. It means the 3D leaf turn runs on laptops too, and record.spec.ts moves with it.
  4. Journey: a board that scales from 1280? Recommended: yes. Tags get about 11% narrower at 1280 (text wraps a little more) and the board reaches about 1680 at 1920.
  5. Contact on a laptop: postcard stacked beside the card? Recommended: yes, as drawn. The alternative carries the tablet arrangement (wide postcard, landscape card under it) up to 1279.
  6. Cats on touch. They rest on entry text at 390 and 430 but never on a control. Recommended: leave them. The alternative is cats that peek over the bottom edge until tapped.
  7. The oak at night under 1024. The trunk nearly disappears into the sky. It is meant to be a silhouette. Recommended: leave it unless it bothers you.
