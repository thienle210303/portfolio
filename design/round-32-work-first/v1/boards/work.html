What the pins say

1. The heading is the Chapter role on the sky, like the Journey's; the lead is my italic. Both are drafts (review page).

2. A, the pass. From 1024 one rail carries the four tickets in a row; below it the pass is a tall back-board with a rail over each ticket. Outdoors a rail needs something to stand on, so A needs a new drawing: a stall's plank on two posts. Thermal paper is light in both themes (the receipt's inks). The tickets are narrow, about 250px at 1440, so the lines run short.

3. B, the bench. Paper lying on the ground, like the postcard and the card, so it needs no support; a binder clip and the pad's title block ("Sheet 1 of 4") are the only new drawing. 2 × 2 from 640, one column below. Wider paper reads better, and the precise grid sits apart from the organic oak that follows. Recommended.

Either way: the whole paper is one link (the title, stretched), named by the title; "Read the whole story" is its visible cue, not a second link. Tone base; no --mark, no --control (the hero's ticket button shares the screen). Server-rendered, no new JavaScript. Each paper is #work-<id>, so old links to a case study land here.
