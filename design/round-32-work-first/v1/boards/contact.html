What the pin says

1. The note: a <textarea> drawn as three ruled lines (it grows if you write more), labelled "A note, if you like", with "A line or two is plenty." under it. Optional, so no *; up to 1,000 characters, and past that one error in my voice: "Keep the note under 1,000 characters." New lines are fine; control characters are not (checked again on the server).

It reaches my inbox inside the lead email, as plain text, below the name and the way back. The thank-you a visitor gets stays word for word what it is: it never repeats the note, the name, or anything typed, so the form still cannot be used to send a stranger text of the sender's choosing.

The lines above stay true ("No message needed." — it isn't; "One postcard, two lines" — the two are still all it takes), so nothing else on Contact changes. CLAUDE.md's non-negotiable becomes "name plus an email or phone, and an optional note".
