Every piece of copy on the site must be Thien leading the story to the visitor, first person. No third person anywhere: chat chips are "What do you do at DoorDash?" not "What does he do…", sample Q "What are your cats called?", "Ask me" not "Ask the site".

**Why:** 2026-10-18, round 21 design review: "every content here must be me leading the story to user and not third pov!"
**How to apply:** when writing or reviewing copy (mockups, src/content, chat corpus answers/suggestions), convert he/his/the site to I/my/me. docs/voice.md already says first person; this extends it to the chat UI. Related: [[round-20-redesign]].
