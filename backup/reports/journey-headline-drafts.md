## Flags

1. **Course name in two forms.** `usc-ta.context`: "Introduction to Computer Concepts & General Programming". `usc-honors-ta.context`: "Introduction to Computer Concepts & General Applications Programming". One is wrong or they are different courses; the content does not say which.
2. **Possible double count (inferred).** `usc-ta` impact "Supported 200+ students during open lab hours" and `usc-honors-ta` responsibilities "lead open lab hours" for a "24+ student honors section". Unknown whether the 24+ are inside the 200+. Both headlines are kept separate so neither adds them.
3. **"Three years" vs dates (minor, inferred).** `eastside-high.context` says "Three years"; `dateRange` "2018 — June 2021" with `sortKey` "2018-12" is about two and a half. Headline avoids the duration.
4. **99.9% in three entries, three meanings.** wordification "validated audio accuracy", schaeffler "accuracy" of fuzzy matching, usc-scraping "success rate". Not a conflict, but two drafted headlines carry it and side by side it can read templated. Schaeffler's headline uses the 96% instead for that reason.
5. **Schaeffler 96% attribution.** The source ties it to "after integrating custom Java functions", not to the paper-to-low-code move. The headline keeps "custom Java" next to the figure; do not shorten it to "low-code lifted throughput 96%".
6. **Demoted entries (not drafted, FYI).** `usc-degree` has the GPA as "GPA 3.8" (context) and "3.8 GPA" (impact), and `graduation.context` repeats it; `code-to-give` has "2nd Place" (role) vs "2nd place." (impact).
7. **Already resolved, no action.** `usc-scraping` start date: the code comment records the résumé's "August 2023" vs the site's "August 2022" and keeps 2022. `fu-of-kyoto` "Summer 2022" vs `endSortKey` "2022-07" is deliberate and commented.
