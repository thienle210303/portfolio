## Principles

- Every word is me talking to you: [docs/voice.md](docs/voice.md).
- Surface vs depth: results at rest, context behind a click.
- Accessibility is a design constraint, not a pass at the end: WCAG 2.2 AA.
- The content layer is the only source of facts; the page never restates one.
